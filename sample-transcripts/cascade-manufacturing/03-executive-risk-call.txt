Rep: At the executive level, what's the concern that keeps this on the
roadmap?

COO: Ransomware. If our plant floor systems went down for even a few days,
we'd miss shipment commitments, and we have penalty clauses in some of our
supply contracts.

Rep: Any customer-facing risk beyond that?

COO: Absolutely — we've had customers audit our security posture before
signing new contracts, and losing one of our larger accounts over a breach
would be a real problem for us, separate from any ransom itself.

Rep: Understood. Anything on the current tooling you want me to know?

COO: Just that our security team is capable, they just don't have the
leverage to keep up with the volume — which is really what pushed us to
start this conversation in the first place.
