Rep: What's the day-to-day pain for your team right now?

IT Director: When something goes wrong on the plant floor, remediation takes
forever, and that's real downtime for our operators — every minute a
workstation is down, production is affected.

Rep: What's a fair cost estimate for that downtime?

IT Director: For a typical employee, we'd put the loaded cost at about 48
dollars an hour.

Rep: And the security team's workload?

IT Director: We've got four analysts, and most of their week goes into
manually investigating alerts that could probably be triaged automatically.
